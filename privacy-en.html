Privacy policy

Updated October 20, 2026 · Developer: Xiaolong Liu

VideoTeach processes your selected videos, drawings, notes and microphone recordings on this device. These files are not sent to a developer server. We do not include advertising, behavioral analytics, face recognition or an account system.

Apple processes payments. VideoTeach uses StoreKit to verify signed purchase and subscription status, including expiry or revocation. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide aggregate store, sales and diagnostic reports under its own policies.

The microphone is requested only when you choose to record. The system photo picker gives access only to videos you select. Export, sharing and backup happen only when you choose them. A receiving app, Files provider or email service processes shared files under its own terms. Obtain permission before sharing someone else’s footage.

Saved reviews remain in the app until you delete them or uninstall it. Temporary imports and exports may be removed during cleanup. There is no developer-operated cloud backup. Export a review backup to a location you control to keep it. You can delete individual reviews or all local reviews in Settings and revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings.

For privacy or support questions, email shawlion@qq.com. If you voluntarily send a support email, the developer receives only what you include and uses it to resolve your request, retaining it only as needed. Do not email footage or other personal information unless it is needed for your request.

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no advertising, analytics or upload forms to this site. Device or Files-provider backups may retain app data according to your own system and service settings.
