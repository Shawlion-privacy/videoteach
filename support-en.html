Quick guide

Updated October 20, 2026 · Developer: Xiaolong Liu

Import a clip from Photos or Files. Choose any sport or add your own category. Use a clear view of the movement; the sport label only organizes your library.

Pause or step through the actual video frames, down to 1/8 speed. The More menu lets you loop a chosen section, inspect the current annotated frame with zoom, or mark key moments. Saved moments appear above the playback controls for quick return.

Record an explanation while playing, pausing, scrubbing and drawing. Finish to save a continuous narration. Re-recording replaces the previous narration. Preview before exporting.

In Pro, select two saved reviews. Move each timeline to the same phase, then link those frames. Both clips use the shared timeline at the selected speed. Comparison is for visual review; it is not a measurement of movement quality.

Use a review’s ••• menu to export a complete .videoteach backup. Restore it from Settings or open the file with VideoTeach. Restoring creates a separate copy and does not overwrite an existing review.

Reviews are local. Removing the app also removes its local data. Save backups somewhere you control. A Pro subscription does not include cloud storage.
